Ways to improve attendance

  • Streak tracking — consecutive present days, shown per student.
  • Recovery target — exact number of classes to attend in a row to get back to 75%.
  • Skip budget — how many classes can still be missed without dropping below 75%.
  • Buddy check-ins — pair low-attendance students with a classmate who reminds them.
  • Weekly digest — send anyone under 80% to a mentor or parent early.
  • Reason tagging — log why a class was missed to spot fixable patterns.
